# Product

## Register

product

## Users

NxtUpdate serves technical macOS users who maintain software installed through several package managers. They use it in a terminal and expect to understand what will change, move quickly through large package lists, and avoid accidental destructive actions.

## Product Purpose

NxtUpdate brings package discovery, review, update, and removal into one predictable workflow. Success means users can assess their system state quickly, choose the right scope, and complete maintenance with trustworthy progress and results.

## Brand Personality

Sober, fast, and reliable. The interface should feel direct and composed, with the legibility and keyboard fluency of a focused macOS utility.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative web-dashboard patterns, noisy status walls, novelty navigation, and visual effects that compete with terminal output. Do not blur the distinction between the NxtUpdate CLI, the Mole CLI, and the separate Mole application.

## Design Principles

- Make the safe path the fastest path.
- Keep state and consequences visible before actions run.
- Use one navigation language across every screen.
- Adapt information density to the terminal instead of truncating meaning.
- Preserve clear boundaries between related tools and external applications.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The interface must remain usable at 80 by 24 cells, never rely on color alone, pair status colors with text or symbols, keep keyboard alternatives visible, and avoid decorative animation. Destructive selections start empty and require explicit confirmation.
